about us.

A bridge between
Chinese talent and European education

The Center of Sino-German Education and Talent Mobility (CSGE) is based in Potsdam, Germany. We offer state-accredited German programs to students in China: you learn 100% online from home, and we build your personal pathway to study and work in Europe.

German UDS teaches and awards the credits and degrees. We take care of everything around them — advice, enrolment, learning support and pathway planning — so you can focus on learning.

Visa support · for M.Sc. students

Study online. Come to Germany when it matters.

During your studies

Short-term visa

We support your application for a short-term visa for stays of up to 90 days — for European campus tours, career weeks, immersive weeks and cultural experiences.

After graduation

Long-term visa

Your German degree needs no recognition process. Use it to apply for a residence permit to look for work in Germany — with part-time work possible while you search.

Visa decisions are made by the German authorities. Requirements include, for example, health insurance and proof of funds — we help you prepare your application.

FAQs.

Questions we hear often

Do I need to come to Germany to start?+

No. All credit courses are fully online, so you can start from China. Onsite events in Potsdam are optional.

Who awards the ECTS credits?+

Our partner university, German UDS, awards all credits and degrees. CSGE advises you, handles your enrolment and supports you while you learn.

Will other universities recognise my ECTS?+

ECTS is the European standard for academic credit. Each receiving university decides whether to transfer credits, and we help you prepare that application.

When do courses start?+

Ed4Change modules start every quarter. The M.Sc. starts in April or October.

Can I visit Europe during my studies?+

Yes. We support your short-term visa application for stays of up to 90 days — for European campus tours, career weeks and cultural experiences. Visa decisions are made by the German authorities.

Can I come to Germany to work after graduating?+

Your German degree needs no further recognition. You can use it to apply for a residence permit to look for work in Germany, during which part-time work is possible. You will need, for example, health insurance and proof of funds — we help you prepare.

Who can apply for the Young M.Sc.?+

Applicants under 27 with a Bachelor’s degree in a STEM subject.

What is your refund policy?+

You can request a refund within 14 days of enrolling.
